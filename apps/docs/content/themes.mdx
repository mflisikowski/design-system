## Independent axes

Brand selects Atlas or Bloom, color scheme selects System, Light, or Dark, and density selects
Comfortable or Compact. Changing one axis never implies a value for another.

## Supported contexts

The token resolver emits Atlas and Bloom across light and dark schemes and both density modes.
System remains a runtime preference that follows the operating-system scheme.

## Production boundary

The Reference CRM brand selector is a demonstration of tenant resolution. Production applications
resolve brand from their tenant before hydration; users are not promised a no-code brand editor.
